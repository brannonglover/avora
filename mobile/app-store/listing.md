## Description (4000 max)

Avora is a browser built around your tabs. Pin the sites you live in, let the rest tidy themselves away, and keep each part of your life in its own Space.

A PLACE FOR EVERY TAB
• Favorites: the handful of sites you open all day, one tap away.
• Pinned: tabs and folders that stay put, day after day.
• Today: everything else. Clear it in one tap when you're done.
• Long press and drag to put any tab, favorite or folder where you want it.

SPACES FOR EVERY PART OF LIFE
Personal, Work, Travel, or whatever you need. Each Space has its own Favorites, pinned tabs and Today list, so switching context is one tap on the Space button at the bottom of the screen.

FIND ANY TAB IN A FEW LETTERS
Quick Nav searches the web and your open tabs at the same time. Start typing and jump straight to the tab you already have open instead of opening it twice.

MADE FOR ONE HAND
• The address bar sits at the bottom, within reach.
• Swipe it left or right to move between tabs.
• Swipe it up to see all your tabs.
• Back and forward, your Space, and New Tab are all in the same bar.

CLOSED IT? GET IT BACK
Close a tab by mistake and Undo brings it right back. The start page also lists recently closed tabs, so you can reopen one in a tap.

THE SAME TABS ON YOUR MAC
Sign in with your email and your Spaces, Favorites, pinned tabs and Today tabs are the same on your iPhone, your iPad and Avora on your Mac. Open a tab on your phone and it's waiting on your Mac. Signing in is optional; without an account, everything stays on this device.

AT HOME ON IPAD
On iPad and in landscape, the toolbar folds into a single row and your tabs sit in a comfortable column, in portrait or landscape.

YOUR DEFAULT BROWSER
Set Avora as your default browser and links from Mail, Messages and other apps open in a new Avora tab.

Avora for Mac is available at avorabrowser.com.
